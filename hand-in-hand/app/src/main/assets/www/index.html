Hand in Hand يَدًا بِيَد

A walking adventure for husband and wife. 30 tasks you can only finish together: one device, one side each.

Abaya colour

How to play

  • Sit together. On a phone or tablet hold it sideways: the left half moves the husband and the right half moves the wife. Drag your thumb to walk, tap ✋ to act.
  • Every task needs both of you: plates that hold doors, heavy chests you push together, lights only one of you can pass, codes one sees and the other enters.
  • Finish a task by standing on the two glowing doorways together.
  • After each task, take a moment for the little “together moment” before you both tap ready.
  • Stuck? Talk it through, then press ↻ to try the task again.
Husband (keyboard)
W A S D to walk
E or Space to act
Wife (keyboard)
Arrow keys to walk
Enter to act

Two game controllers work too.
