Rooftop Quickscope

Walk a war-torn city block, climb the rooftops, and land trick shots. Solo, soldiers hunt you through the streets. Online, your friends are the enemy.

Loadout

Controls

  • Mouse aim, left-click fire
  • Right-click / F scope
  • W A S D walk
  • Shift sprint, or hold breath in scope
  • C crouch   Space jump
  • Q / E fast spin for 360s
  • X or wheel swap (twice = Y-Y)
  • Esc free the mouse to leave
  • Left side drag to walk
  • Right side drag to aim
  • Scope hold, drag it to aim, lift to fire
  • Spin / Jump / Y-Y buttons

Trick shots

  • Quickscope x1.5
  • No scope x2
  • Jump shot off a roof or a hop x1.5
  • 360 / 720 / 1080 x3 / x5 / x8
  • Y-Y x1.5
  • Headshot, wallbang x1.5
  • Longshot over 60 m x1.25
  • Collateral x2
  • Hardscope x0.5
